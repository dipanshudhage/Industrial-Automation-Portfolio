# EKTS — Electrical Control Simulations

**Electrical Control Systems | Motor Control | Industrial Automation**

A technical collection of electrical control circuit simulations developed using **Electrical Control Techniques Simulator (EKTS)** to study switching devices, control logic, and motor-control methods used in industrial applications.

## Overview

This repository documents practical simulation exercises focused on the fundamentals of electrical control engineering. It demonstrates the operating principles of relays and contactors, motor direction control, and star–delta motor starting.

The project forms part of my ongoing learning in **Industrial Automation**, with an emphasis on understanding conventional electrical control systems and building a foundation for PLC-based automation.

## Software & Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| EKTS | Electrical control circuit simulation |

## Simulation Modules

### 01. Contactor Control
Study of contactor coil operation and contact switching in electrical control circuits.

### 02. Relay Control
Simulation of relay energization and NO/NC contact operation to understand basic control logic.

### 03. Direct Drive Control
Study of direct motor-control operation and the associated control-circuit principles.

### 04. Forward–Reverse Motor Control
Simulation of forward and reverse motor operation using contactors and interlocking principles to prevent conflicting commands.

### 05. Star–Delta Motor Starting
Study of star–delta starting, including the star-starting phase and transition to delta operation for reduced motor starting current.
